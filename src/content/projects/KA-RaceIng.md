Elected **Head of Aerodynamics** for KA RaceIng, leading the aerodynamic department in the development of a high-performance electric Formula Student race car. I was responsible for technical strategy, team coordination, and execution under intense competition timelines.

During the season, KA RaceIng achieved a **world ranking of #2 in Autonomous** and **#10 in Electric**.

## Responsibilities & Impact

### Team Leadership
- **Led a team of 13 engineers**, structuring workflows across aerodynamics, CFD, and lightweight structures.
- Defined clear technical ownership, milestones, and development priorities for the whole season (1 year).
- Built a highly motivated, performance-driven team culture and made lots of lasting friends.

### Technical Direction
- Defined and executed the aerodynamic development roadmap in alignment with overall vehicle performance targets and regulatory constraints.
- Supervised CFD methodology, validation strategy, and data-driven design decisions.
- Coordinated closely with chassis, powertrain, and autonomous systems to ensure seamless integration of LiDAR and sensor packaging.
- Achieved **>30% weight reduction** and **>20% aerodynamic performance improvement** compared to the baseline vehicle.

### Execution & Operations
- Oversaw in-house manufacturing of the complete aerodynamic package, ensuring quality, repeatability, and deadline compliance.
- Organized intensive production phases, working in rotating shifts from early morning to midnight during critical build periods.
- Managed communication with sponsors, suppliers, and the university to secure materials, manufacturing resources, and institutional support.

This role strengthened my ability to combine **technical development, operational discipline, and structured leadership**, delivering measurable performance gains in a highly competitive engineering environment.
