Comprehensive review of *PDE-Transformer: Efficient and Versatile Transformers for Physics Simulations*, analyzing its architectural innovations for large-scale PDE modeling.

The work investigates how multi-scale U-shaped transformers, shifted window attention, and the Separate Channel embedding strategy address scalability and multi-physics challenges in scientific machine learning.

Additionally, I conducted independent experiments to evaluate the model’s transferability and performance on complex physics systems, assessing stability, generalization, and efficiency compared to existing baselines.
