cfd rans leadership composites

##The role

As Head of Aerodynamics I led a team of 13 engineers through a complete aerodynamic development cycle: concept, CFD-driven design, wind-tunnel-free validation, composite manufacturing, and on-track correlation. The job was equal parts engineering and coordination — keeping simulation, design, and manufacturing deadlines aligned across two seasons.

##Engineering

  • +14% downforce. Designed and optimized the full aero package using RANS-based CFD with the k-ω SST turbulence model.
  • Trustworthy simulations. Improved mesh generation and boundary-layer resolution (y+ control), and conducted grid convergence studies so design decisions rested on numbers we could defend.
  • CFD → carbon. Carried designs through composite optimization and manufacturing — the loop closed only when parts survived on track.
